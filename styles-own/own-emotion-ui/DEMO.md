| world | time | what happens | patterns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 stage | 0–6 s | hero capsule under a spotlight; eyes pop, gaze lines lock, brackets snap, 紧张 bar rises, callout 表情自然吗？; caption 你以为，全场都在盯着你？ | M1 M2 M5 P8 |
| 2 bedroom | 6–12 s | sitter on the bed at night, fast wall clock, thought cloud replays the stage, 脑内重播 tally, flip clock 01:17, dive into the cloud | P6 P7 M9 |
| 3 experiment | 12–18 s | two lamps flicker on; a kid in an embarrassing T-shirt walks in; cards 46 % (believed) vs 23 % (actual), chip 高估了一倍; cite Gilovich 等 2000 | M6 P5 |
| 4 stage again | 18–24 s | gaze released, crowd phones light up, spotlight dims, meter drops, hero takes out a phone; crowd callout 我刚才说错话没？; chip 你没那么显眼 | M3 P9 M5 |
Fact check: Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), "The spotlight effect in social judgment", JPSP 78(2):211–222 — students in a Barry Manilow T-shirt estimated ~46 % of peers noticed; ~23 % actually did.
